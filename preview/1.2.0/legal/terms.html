If the App Store does not provide a custom license agreement applicable to you, the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) applies. This page explains Pro purchases and subscriptions; it does not replace or amend that license.

Free use and Pro benefits

You do not need to buy Pro to use the app’s available free photo and video organization features. Where daily allowances apply, the photo allowance is counted at the first actual organizing action in a new group, and videos when a previously unwatched video starts playing in BlindBox, rather than by recording date. Continuing an existing group or replaying watched videos does not use another new-item allowance. Whether allowances apply, the actual remainder and reset time are shown in the app.

Confirmed, active Monthly, Annual and Lifetime Pro provide the same full Pro benefits: no daily free new-group/new-video allowance restrictions, adjustable photo-group size, custom themes and additional app icons that are available. The benefits described in the version at purchase determine the available capabilities, which still require the relevant system permissions and device support. Unreleased features are not already-delivered capabilities.

Plans, prices and payment

Plans may include Monthly Pro (one month, auto-renewable), Annual Pro (one year, auto-renewable), and Lifetime Pro (a one-time, non-consumable purchase without automatic renewal). Available plans and local prices are those shown by the app’s store offers and Apple’s final confirmation. This page does not fix prices. Apple’s confirmation shows the applicable currency, full billing period and charge.

A purchase is initiated only when you confirm through Apple’s purchase flow. Apple bills your Apple Account. Auto-renewable plans renew under Apple’s rules unless future renewal is cancelled through Apple. Check Apple’s confirmation, notices and subscription management for the next charge, cancellation arrangements and price changes.

An active non-consumable Lifetime Pro purchase has no scheduled expiry and is not a subscription. It does not make every future separately paid product free or promise that the app, operating system or third-party services will remain available forever. Statutory protections remain unaffected.

Changing plans and buying Lifetime Pro

For plans in the same subscription group and service level with different durations, switching between monthly and annual generally takes effect at the next renewal. Apple’s confirmation determines the actual effective date, charge and adjustments. Selecting another plan does not itself mean an immediate upgrade or payment has occurred.

Buying Lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing monthly or annual subscription or refund subscription charges. Check and manage Apple subscriptions you no longer need before or after purchase to avoid continued renewal. You can leave the current purchase and manage your subscription first.

Checking benefits and restoring purchases

If you previously purchased but benefits are missing, use the Apple Account that made the purchase and choose Restore Purchases in My Benefits or the purchase page. Restoration does not itself buy again. Results depend on Apple’s actual valid purchase records and may require a network connection or Apple authentication. Restoration is not guaranteed for another account or a refunded purchase.

A pending approval, cancellation, failure or future plan that has not taken effect is not a new active Pro purchase. A temporary inability to check records is also not confirmation that you have no benefits; retry or restore as shown in the app. Activation, expiry, refunds and revocations follow the actual valid status.

Cancellation, expiry and refunds

You can manage future renewal in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See Apple’s cancellation instructions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancelling renewal normally leaves the current paid, valid period available; refunds, revocations or other effective Apple status changes are separate. Follow the system’s next-renewal date and confirmation. This page does not impose a universal 24-hour cancellation cutoff.

Cancellation and refunds are separate. Some purchases may qualify for a refund; follow Apple’s refund instructions to apply to Apple. Eligibility, decisions, amounts and payment timing depend on Apple’s handling and applicable law. Approval is not guaranteed. This page does not exclude or limit non-excludable statutory consumer rights.

Expiry of one subscription or gift does not itself remove another independent, active benefit. Without another active Pro source, the free features then available apply. A separately confirmed legacy photo benefit does not grant full Pro video, theme or icon benefits.

Free gifts and unavailable offers

Legacy-user free-gift activation is not currently available. This page does not promise gift eligibility, duration or an activation date, or include an unavailable gift as a guarantee attached to a paid plan. Any future gift needs its own clear eligibility, scope and duration. A gift is not a paid subscription and does not renew itself.
